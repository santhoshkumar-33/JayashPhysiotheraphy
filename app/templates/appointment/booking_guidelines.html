Appointment Information

Before You Visit

A few simple steps can help us provide the best possible care during your consultation.

Arrive Early

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before your scheduled appointment to complete any necessary formalities.

Bring Previous Reports

Carry relevant medical records, scans, X-rays or prescriptions, if available.

Wear Comfortable Clothing

Comfortable clothing helps our physiotherapists assess movement and perform treatment effectively.

Home Visits Available

Home physiotherapy services are available for patients who are unable to visit the clinic.
